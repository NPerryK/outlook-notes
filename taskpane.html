    Link with My Notes (Outlook)

    My Notes shows the CDF status on every email that mentions a CDF, and your Outlook notes on a CDF show here in the CDF lookup. The data moves by copy and paste — nothing goes through GitHub.

    ⇢
    To Outlook
    The status of every CDF in this workbook.

    Then in Outlook: open My Notes on any email, click an empty spot in it and press Ctrl+V (or Reports → CDF status → paste in the box).

    From Outlook
    When you paste the status in My Notes, it copies your notes back right away — then press Ctrl+V anywhere in this pane (or in the box). Or in My Notes: Reports → CDF status → Copy notes for CDF Updater.
